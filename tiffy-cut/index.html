Quick Install on macOS

1
Download tiffy-cut-Installer.dmg and double-click to mount it.
2
Drag tiffy-cut.app into your Applications folder.
3
First-Time Launch (Gatekeeper): Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway, or run:
xattr -cr /Applications/tiffy-cut.app
