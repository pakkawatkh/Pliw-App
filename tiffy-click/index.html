Minimal Menu Bar controls, zero interference with links or tabs, and full Thai/English support.

🫧

Apple Liquid Glass 360° Orb

Compact 28pt frosted glass orb with a slender rim-hugging chevron that orbits 360° toward your cursor while scrolling — and disappears when idle.

🎯

Browser-Only & Link-Safe

Activates only on empty webpage areas in Chrome, Safari, Brave, Arc, Edge, and Firefox. Links, tabs, buttons, and non-browser apps stay 100% standard middle-click.

🖐️

2–10 Finger Trackpad & Drag

Click or tap with 2–10 fingers, Palm Rejection edge filtering, plus proper otherMouseDragged support for panning in Figma, Blender, and CAD.

🔄

Floating Glass Update Toast

Auto-fading macOS glass notification when up to date, and a one-click Update button that downloads, installs, and relaunches automatically.

Quick Install on macOS (วิธีติดตั้งบน macOS)

1
Download tiffy-click-Installer.dmg and double-click to mount it.
2
Drag tiffy-click.app into your Applications folder.
3
First-Time Launch (Gatekeeper): Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway, or run:
xattr -cr /Applications/tiffy-click.app
4
Enable tiffy-click in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
